משנים חיים, שעה אחת בשבועChanging Lives, One Hour at a Time

מאז 1999, ארגון נתיבות מחבר אלפי יהודים למסורת שלהם — בלי לחץ, בלי עלות, ובלי שיפוטיות. מאחורי כל מספר יש אדם אמיתי שגילה מחדש את החיבור לשורשים שלו.Since 1999, Netivot Organization has been connecting thousands of Jewish people to their heritage — free of charge, free of pressure, and free of judgment. Behind every number is a real person who rediscovered their connection to their roots.

מה שמייחד אותנוWhat Makes Us Different

גישה ייחודית ללימוד יהדותA Unique Approach to Jewish Learning

🤝 אישי ומותאם🤝 Personal and Tailored

כל לומד מקבל חברותא שמותאמת לנושא, לרמה ולאישיות שלו. זה לא שיעור — זו שיחה אישית בגובה העיניים.Every learner is matched with a chavruta (study partner) suited to their interests, level, and personality. This is not a lecture — it is a personal, eye-level conversation. Our matching process takes into account the learner's background, areas of interest, preferred language, and schedule. Whether someone wants to explore the meaning of Shabbat, understand the structure of Jewish prayer, or dive deep into Talmudic reasoning, we find the right partner for them. Many of our learners describe the experience as having a knowledgeable friend who is always happy to share — without any agenda or expectation.

💰 חינם לחלוטין💰 Completely Free

הלימוד הוא 100% בחינם, ללא התחייבות. אנחנו מאמינים שתורה שייכת לכולם.All learning sessions are 100% free of charge, with no commitment required. We believe that Torah belongs to everyone. Netivot Organization has operated on this principle since its founding in 1999: access to Jewish wisdom should never depend on someone's ability to pay. Our entire operation is funded by generous donors who share this vision. Learners can start, pause, or stop at any time — there is never any financial obligation, enrollment fee, or hidden cost.

📱 נגיש ונוח📱 Accessible and Convenient

לימוד בטלפון, בזום, או פנים אל פנים — שעה בשבוע, בזמן שנוח לכם.Learning takes place by phone, Zoom, or face-to-face at a local synagogue — one hour per week, at whatever time works best for the learner. We understand that modern life is busy, and that not everyone can attend a fixed class at a set location. That is why we bring the learning to you. Our flexible format has allowed us to reach Jews across Israel, the United States, Europe, and beyond.

🤖 משה גינזי AI🤖 Moshe Ginzy AI Assistant

הצ'אטבוט שלנו עונה על שאלות ביהדות 24/7 — בעברית ובאנגלית, עם קול אמיתי.Our AI-powered chatbot, Moshe Ginzy, is available around the clock to answer any question about Judaism — in Hebrew and English, with a real voice. Moshe Ginzy combines cutting-edge artificial intelligence with a carefully curated knowledge base of over 16,500 Jewish teachings, halacha, traditions, and philosophy. The chatbot serves as a gateway: many users who start with a casual question end up requesting a personal chavruta to continue their learning journey in depth.

איך זה עובדHow It Works

המסלול מהשאלה הראשונה ללימוד קבועThe Journey from First Question to Ongoing Learning

① גילוי① Discovery

אנשים מגלים אותנו דרך פייסבוק, אפליקציית משה גינזי, או חברים. אנחנו פוגשים אנשים איפה שהם נמצאים.People discover us through Facebook advertising, the Moshe Ginzy app, word-of-mouth referrals from friends and family, or organic web searches. Our digital outreach strategy uses social media to reach secular Jewish audiences who might not otherwise seek out Jewish learning. We meet people where they are — scrolling through their feed — and offer a low-pressure invitation to explore their heritage.

② שיחה ראשונה② First Conversation

נציג שלנו מתקשר, מכיר את הלומד, ומתאים חברותא מושלמת בהתאם לנושא, לרמה ולאישיות.A Netivot representative calls the interested person for a friendly introductory conversation. This is not a sales call — it is a genuine effort to understand what the person is curious about, what their background is, and what format would work best for them. Based on this conversation, we carefully match them with a chavruta partner who shares relevant interests and can communicate in their preferred language and style.

③ לימוד שוטף③ Ongoing Learning

שעה בשבוע של שיחה אישית על נושאים שמעניינים את הלומד — בטלפון, בזום, או פנים אל פנים.The learner and their chavruta meet for one hour each week — by phone, Zoom, or in person. Topics are chosen by the learner and can range from weekly Torah portions to Jewish philosophy, practical halacha, holiday preparation, or any other area of interest. The sessions are conversational and exploratory, not lecture-based. Over time, many learners describe a transformation in how they see themselves, their heritage, and their connection to the Jewish community.

④ צמיחה④ Growth and Community

הרבה לומדים מתחילים לשמור שבת, ללכת לבית כנסת, ואפילו ללמד אחרים.Many learners who started with a single question go on to attend synagogue regularly, observe Shabbat, celebrate holidays with deeper understanding, and even begin teaching their own family members and friends. Some have described a ripple effect — their curiosity inspiring their spouse, their children, and their social circle. This organic, pressure-free growth is at the heart of what Netivot does: planting seeds and letting each person grow at their own pace.

ההתפשטות שלנוOur Global Reach

מחברים יהודים בכל מקוםConnecting Jews Everywhere

ארגון נתיבות פועל בישראל, ארצות הברית, אירופה ומעבר לים. אנחנו מחברים יהודים מכל הרקעים — חילונים, מסורתיים, ואנשים שרק רוצים להבין את המסורת שלהם. הדבר היחיד שצריך זה סקרנות.Netivot Organization operates across Israel, the United States, Europe, and beyond. We connect Jews from all backgrounds — secular, traditional, and people who simply want to understand their own heritage better. The only requirement is curiosity. Our network of volunteer chavrutas includes rabbis, educators, professionals, and community leaders who donate their time because they believe in the mission. Each chavruta partner undergoes a personal introduction process to ensure they can provide a warm, patient, and knowledgeable learning experience. The result is a global web of Jewish learning that operates without buildings, without tuition, and without barriers — just two people, one hour, and a shared love of wisdom.
